Getting started

  1. Log in with the username and password given to you. Your account is locked to the first computer you log in from. If you must use another computer, ask the manager or admin to reset your device. Click the eye icon in the password box to see what you typed. Three wrong passwords in a row lock the account; the admin, or your branch manager if they may add cashiers, can unlock it from the Users page.
  2. Choose your billing counter on the next screen. Every bill you make is numbered under that counter. You can change counter from the top bar.
  3. The left menu holds everything. Click the arrows at the top of the menu to shrink it to icons. On a phone, tap the menu button in the top bar.
  4. Light or dark theme: click the moon / sun button in the top bar to switch. The choice is remembered on this computer and browser.
  5. Notice popups appear when the office sends you a notice. Read it and click "I have read this". It will keep coming back until you do.
  6. Log out from the bottom of the menu when you leave the counter. Your login and logout times are recorded.
  7. To change your password, click the key icon at the bottom of the menu.

Billing a customer

  1. Open Billing. The cursor is already in the search box.
  2. Type part of the product name, pick it with the arrow keys, press Enter. The item is added with quantity 1 and the cursor jumps to the quantity box.
  3. With a barcode scanner: click in the search box once, then scan the packet. The item is added with quantity 1 and the cursor stays in the search box, so scan the next packet straight away. Scanning the same packet again adds one more. A scan also works while the cursor is in a quantity box. If nothing is added, the packet's barcode is not saved on the product yet: a manager can add it under Products > Edit.
  4. Type the quantity (decimals allowed for loose items, e.g. 0.250) and press Enter to return to search. Press F2 any time to jump back to search.
  5. Items with a live offer show an OFFER tag; the saving is applied automatically and shown in the totals.
  6. Optional: pick a customer by name or phone, or click + to add one. Needed only for credit bills or when the customer wants their name on the bill.
  7. Choose payment: Cash, UPI, Card or Credit. For cash you can type the amount received to see the change to return.
  8. Press F9 or click Save & Print. The bill opens in a new tab in the format set for your branch (A4 or thermal) and the print dialog appears.

Prices cannot be changed on the bill. {% if role == 'cashier' %}If a price is wrong, tell your manager.{% else %}If a price is wrong, correct it under Products > Edit, or record the new purchase price under Purchases; the next bill uses the new price. Use the Discount box or an Offer for a special price.{% endif %} A manual discount can be typed in the Discount box. {% if is_admin %}Only admin can cancel a saved bill (Bills page), which restores stock.{% else %}If a bill was made by mistake, ask the admin to cancel it.{% endif %}

Reprint: Bills page, find the bill, click Print (branch format), 80 (thermal) or A4.

Customers and udhaar (credit)

  • Credit bill: on the billing screen choose Credit, select or add the customer, enter the amount paid now. The rest goes to the customer's udhaar. The bill prints the previous balance, this bill's credit and the total due.
  • Collecting money: Customers > click the customer > Receive payment. Enter the amount, mode and optionally which invoice it is for (otherwise oldest first). Click Save & print receipt. A numbered receipt voucher prints listing the invoices it settled and the balance still due.
  • The customer page shows Unpaid invoices and a full Statement with every bill and receipt by number. The Receipts menu lists all receipts for the branch.
  • {% if is_admin %}Admin can cancel a receipt from the Receipts page; the amount becomes due again. A bill with a receipt applied cannot be cancelled until that receipt is cancelled.{% else %}If a receipt was entered wrongly, ask the admin to cancel it.{% endif %}

Products and stock

  • Products lists every item with unit, HSN, GST rate, cost, selling price, MRP and current stock for your branch. Use the search box and category filter.
  • Stock shows quantities, alert levels, cost and sell price per item, and the total stock value. Tick "Low stock only" to see items at or below their alert level; the dashboard also counts them.
    • {% if is_mgr %}
  • Add or edit a product: Products > Add Product or Edit. Selling price includes GST, the way MRP works. Set the low stock alert level per item. Click in the Barcode box and scan the packet (or type the number printed under the bars); the cashier can then scan it at billing. One barcode belongs to one product.
  • Adjust on the Stock page is only for corrections: damaged, expired or a counting difference. Use a negative number to remove stock and always type a reason. Stock history shows every adjustment.
  • Transfer to branch moves stock from your branch to another and records it on both sides.
  • Bought new stock, maybe at a new price? Do not use Adjust. Record a Purchase.
    • {% else %}
  • You can view products and stock but not change them. Report wrong stock or prices to your manager.
    • {% endif %}

Barcode scanner

Any USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner works. It behaves like a keyboard: it types the number printed under the bars and then presses Enter. Nothing needs to be installed or set up in the system.

Scanning at the billing counter

  1. Open Billing and click in the search box once (or press F2).
  2. Scan the packet. The item appears on the bill with quantity 1, the row flashes green, and the cursor stays in the search box.
  3. Scan the next packet. Scanning the same packet again adds one more of it. For loose items or large quantities, click the quantity box and type the number.
  4. A scan also works while the cursor is in a quantity or price box: the code is taken out of that box and the item is added.
  5. Finish the bill as usual with F9.
{% if is_mgr %}

Saving the barcode on a product

  1. Open Products, click Add Product or Edit on the item.
  2. Click in the Barcode box and scan the packet. The number appears and the cursor moves to the next box. You can also type the number printed under the bars.
  3. Save. From now on scanning that packet at billing adds this product.
  4. One barcode belongs to one product. If the number is already saved on another product, the form says which one; scan the right packet or clear the box.
  5. The Products list shows the barcode under each name. The Products, Stock and purchase search boxes find items by barcode too, so you can scan a packet to check its stock or add it to a purchase.

Items without a printed barcode (loose rice, vegetables, bakery) are billed by name as before. If you print your own labels, type the number from the label into the Barcode box.

{% else %}

If a scan adds nothing, the barcode is not saved on that product yet. Bill it by name and tell your manager, who can add the barcode under Products.

{% endif %}

If scanning does not work

  • Nothing happens: the cursor was not in the search box. Press F2 and scan again.
  • "No product found": the barcode is not saved on the product yet{% if is_mgr %}; add it under Products > Edit{% endif %}.
  • The number appears in the box but the item is not added: the scanner is not sending Enter after the code. Most scanners have a setting sheet in the box; scan the "Add Enter / CR suffix" code on it.
  • Wrong characters appear: the scanner is set to another keyboard language. Set it to US English keyboard from its setting sheet.

Purchases (stock in)

  1. Purchases > New Purchase (also the "+ Purchase (stock in)" button on the Stock page).
  2. Choose the supplier, type the supplier's bill number and date.
  3. Type each product name and press Enter; enter the quantity and the unit cost you paid. Optionally type a New sell price. Keep "Update cost?" ticked so the product's purchase price becomes this cost.
  4. Attach the supplier's bill (PDF or photo). This is required; the purchase cannot be saved without it.
  5. Click Save purchase. Stock goes up at your branch. The bill copy can be viewed or replaced later from the purchase page.

Suppliers are managed under the Suppliers menu (name, phone, GSTIN, address).

Offers

  • Offers > New offer. Types: % off a product, special price for a product, buy X get Y free, % off a category, % off the bill above an amount, flat amount off the bill above an amount.
  • Set the dates. {% if is_admin %}Admin can choose all branches or one branch.{% else %}Your offers apply to your branch only; offers made by admin for all branches also apply.{% endif %}
  • Live offers apply automatically at billing. When several match one item the customer gets the best one; a bill offer applies after item offers. The bill prints the offer name and "You saved".
  • Switch an offer off, edit it, or delete it from the list. The list shows Live, Upcoming, Expired or Off.

Branch messages and stock requests

  • Request stock: Branch messages > Request stock. Type the product; the form shows how much every branch has. Enter the quantity and send.
  • The receiving branch opens the request and clicks Send stock, which transfers the quantity immediately and records it in stock history on both sides, or Decline with a reason. The requester can reopen or close the thread.

My documents

  • Your offer letter, ID card and monthly salary slips appear under My documents as PDFs. You get a notice when a new one is issued. View, print or download them any time.
  • Offer letter: print it, sign it, take a photo or scan, and upload it in the "Signed copy" column. The office is notified.
  • Your details (employee ID, designation, joining date) are shown there. If anything is wrong, tell your manager or the admin.

Staff HR documents

  1. Staff HR lists {{ 'all staff' if is_admin else 'the cashiers of your branch' }}. Open a person's Profile and fill designation, phone, address, date of birth, blood group, photo, salary (basic, HRA, allowances), bank details, PAN and emergency contact. The employee ID is generated automatically.
  2. Offer letter: one click; built from the template{% if is_admin %} under Settings{% endif %} with the person's details. When the staff member uploads the signed copy you are notified and it shows against the letter; you can also file a scan yourself.

  4. Salary slip: choose the month. Earnings are prefilled from the profile and unpaid leave days are counted from approved leave. Adjust figures, add incentive or deductions, and generate. The slip shows net pay in words.

Every document is saved on the person's page and the staff member sees it under My documents.

Users

    {% if is_admin %}
  • Add a user: Users > fill username, name, role (cashier, manager, admin), branch, designation (dropdown), joining date and password. The employee ID is assigned automatically.
  • Permissions for managers: create cashier users; reset device locks; manage HR documents; view analytics. Tick what each manager may do.
  • Device lock: on by default for cashiers and managers; the first login binds the computer. Untick to allow any computer.
  • Login lock: three wrong passwords in a row lock any account. A locked user appears in a red banner at the top of the Users page; click Unlock there. Managers allowed to add cashiers can unlock their own branch's cashiers. Admin accounts unlock by themselves after 15 minutes.
  • Untick Active to stop someone logging in without deleting their history.
    • {% else %}
  • Cashier users: you can add cashiers for your branch, change their name, reset their password, set the joining date, and deactivate them. Role and branch are fixed.
  • Locked cashier: three wrong passwords lock a cashier out. A red banner at the top of Cashier users names them; click Unlock and they can try again.
    • {% endif %}

Device locks

  • Each locked account shows the registered computer address and when it was bound, plus recent blocked login attempts.
  • Click Reset device when a computer is replaced or its address changed. The next login registers the new machine.
  • Give each counter PC a fixed address in the router (DHCP reservation) so the lock stays reliable.

Settings

  • Shop details printed on every bill and report: name, tagline, address, phone, GSTIN, state. With a GSTIN the bill says TAX INVOICE, otherwise CASH MEMO.
  • Allow billing when stock is zero: normally off, so a bill is refused when stock is insufficient.
  • Staff settings: employee ID prefix, ID card validity years, designation list.
  • Bill printing: company default format (A4, thermal 80 mm, thermal 58 mm). Branches can override.
  • Login page background: upload a photo for the login screen.
  • Authorised signature: upload a scanned signature or create one from a typed name in a handwriting style. It prints on bills, receipts, offer letters, salary slips and ID cards with the signatory's name.
  • Offer letter template with placeholders such as {name}, {designation}, {salary}.
  • Leave policy (separate page) for entitlement per leave type.

Branches, counters, printing, signatures

  • Branches & counters: add a branch with a short code (used in invoice numbers), address, phone, own GSTIN if different, and its bill format. Each branch gets Counter 1; add more counters so several cashiers can bill at once, each with its own bill number sequence.
  • Branch signatures: give a branch its own authorised signature and signatory name (upload or create from a name). Its bills, receipts and staff documents use it; otherwise the company signature is used.
  • Thermal printer: install the printer's Windows driver, set the paper width, and in the browser print dialog choose the printer with margins None and scale 100%. Set the branch's bill format to the matching thermal size.
  • Other PCs in the shop open the system at the main PC's address, for example http://192.168.1.5:5000.

Activity log

  • Every action by every user is recorded: bills, cancellations, purchases, stock changes, price edits, payments, settings, users, notices, leave decisions, logins, failed and blocked logins, prints and exports. Passwords are never stored.
  • Default is today; pick a date range, filter by staff or branch, search by text such as a bill number, and export.

Backup and GitHub

  • All data is in one file, rktraders.db, and uploaded files (supplier bills, leave documents, staff documents, photos, signatures) are in the uploads folder, both inside the project folder. Copy both to a pen drive or cloud folder every day.
  • The program code is on GitHub. It never contains the database or uploads. After editing code yourself, double-click push.bat to upload the change.
  • To start the system: double-click start.bat. Keep its window open while the shop is running.

Common questions

Your account is bound to the first computer you used. Ask the {{ 'manager or admin' if role == 'cashier' else 'admin' }} to reset your device, then log in again from this computer.
The bill did not print.
Open Bills, find the bill and click Print again. Check the printer is on and selected in the print dialog.
I scanned a packet but nothing was added.
Press F2 so the cursor is in the search box and scan again. If it says "No product found", the barcode is not saved on the product yet{{ ': ask your manager to add it.' if role == 'cashier' else ': open Products > Edit, click in the Barcode box and scan.' }} See the Barcode scanner section above.
The product is not found while billing.
Try a shorter part of the name. If it is a new item, {{ 'ask your manager to add it under Products.' if role == 'cashier' else 'add it under Products > Add Product.' }}
"Only X in stock" when billing.
The system has less stock than you are selling. {{ 'Tell your manager to check the stock.' if role == 'cashier' else 'Record the purchase or correct the stock with Adjust, then bill again.' }}
A customer paid old udhaar.
Customers > the customer > Receive payment > Save & print receipt. Give them the receipt.
The popup notice keeps coming back.
Click "I have read this" on each notice. "Remind me later" only hides it for the current screen.
